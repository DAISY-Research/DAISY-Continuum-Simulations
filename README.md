# DAISY Continuum Simulations

**DAISY Research Group | The Architectural Limit of Reality**

This repository contains numerical simulations and computational models testing the **Unified Energy Continuum** hypothesis. 

## Theoretical Framework
The foundational architecture for these simulations is described in the preprint:
**"The Architectural Limit of Reality: Unified Energy Continuum Model of Particle Formation, Gravitation, and Cosmological Evolution"** (Roman Goncharenko, 2026).
*Link to the full paper: (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18727813).

## Objective
The primary objective is to determine whether nonlinear field systems admit stable, localized, self-sustaining configurations (particles) consistent with the field synchronization mechanism.

We are modeling the minimal nonlinear wave equation (Section 11.2):
**∂²ψ/∂t² - ∇²ψ + α|ψ|²ψ + β|ψ|⁴ψ = 0**

Our goal is to demonstrate the spontaneous transition from propagating waves to stable, closed energy nodes.

## Call to the Community
We welcome contributions from computational physicists and data scientists to optimize the finite-difference solvers and explore the parameter space (α, β) where stable localized solitons emerge.
